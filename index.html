Grant life

The docket in sequence

  1. 01

    Create

    Funder names the builder and locks the grant terms with two public specification pages.

  2. 02

    Fund

    Reserve the next tranche in order, with a milestone date and GEN value attached.

  3. 03

    Evidence

    Builder attaches two public evidence pages from different hosts, one for each milestone.

  4. 04

    Review

    Anyone opens the tranche for adjudication once both evidence URLs are live.

  5. 05

    Release

    YES pays the builder; NO closes and refunds; unknown or disagree reopens the rung.

Docket notes

Common questions

Why two sources?

Each milestone needs independent corroboration from different hosts before a release can clear.

Who can review?

Any party can open review when the grant is active and both evidence URLs are present.

What happens on timeout?

After the review window expires, the tranche reopens as reserved, and the funder may also claw back.
